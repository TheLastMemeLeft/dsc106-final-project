Sky

Where the rain is heading

Change in rainfall compared to 1995-2014, averaged over 8 climate models. Use the slider to move through time and switch between low and high emissions. Click the map, then scroll to zoom.

Data: CMIP6 precipitation, 8 models, 5-year averages, 4° grid. Grey areas are deserts (under 0.25 mm of rain a day), where a % change isn't meaningful.

Land

What we put into the air

CO₂ in the atmosphere, driven by human emissions. Up to 2014 this is the historical run. After that it splits into a low and a high emissions scenario.

Data: GFDL-ESM4 co2mass, converted to ppm. Scenarios: SSP1-2.6 (low), SSP5-8.5 (high).

More CO₂, warmer oceans

Each dot is one year, plotted by CO₂ level and average sea surface temperature. Hover over a dot to see the year.

Data: GFDL-ESM4, global area-weighted mean sea surface temperature.

Ocean surface

Sea surface temperature, 1950-2100

Press play or drag the slider. Switch to "Change" to see how much each area has warmed since 1950-1979.

Data: GFDL-ESM4 tos (sea surface temperature), yearly mean, 2° grid. Years after 2014 are projections.

How much of the ocean has warmed

Share of ocean area by how much it has warmed since 1950-1979. This follows the year and scenario picked on the map above. The dashed line is 1980.

Deep ocean

Sea level rise from warming water

Water expands as it warms, so sea level goes up even without melting ice. The shaded band is the range across models.

Data: CMIP6 zostoga (global thermosteric sea level), relative to 1995-2014.
